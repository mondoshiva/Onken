One system for the full camp operation. From first inquiry to check-out, the platform connects bookings, boats, payments, guests, tasks and revenue in a clear operational workspace.

## 5) Problem/Solution
**Problems:** Double bookings, unclear payment status, scattered customer data, limited revenue visibility, manual seasonal planning.  
**Solution:** Central availability, booking and payment in one place, simple dashboards, structured guest management, better operational control.

## 6) Feature Grid
- Booking management: Handle requests, reservations and capacity from one flow.
- Payment tracking: Keep deposits and final balances transparent.
- Revenue dashboard: Monitor performance by unit, boat, period or segment.
- Boat rental: Manage fleet availability, handovers and operations.
- Boat service & maintenance: Keep maintenance schedules on track.
- Availability calendar: View cabins and boats in one timeline.
- CRM & customer profiles: Centralize guest history and preferences.
- Customer segmentation: Identify booking patterns and key groups.
- Check-in & guest data: Prepare arrivals with structured data.
- Seasonal planning: Plan resources before peak demand hits.
- Tasks & checklists: Standardize workflows across the team.
- Partner and owner overviews: Share clear operational reporting.
